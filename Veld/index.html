§XIV Running a Veld

The Veld in Motion

Every point of light is a Solo, held by its Zelf. Its Trek reaches every other it perceives, and each look it takes is a Vermenigvuldiging: the other's Eigen as it stood a Vertraging ago, through the Vervorming of the look. Where its pull reaches one most, it aims, as Zin, and where two aims answer, each as the other reads it, a Relatie forms. Every crossing of a Drempel is a Vonk, traded at once with the Medium's Warmte. Stilte's Trek wears what is held at every look a Zelf takes at itself. When a Relatie ends, its Echoes do not: they stay, as Rouw.

More settings, and presets

One Octaaf differs from another in what its Octaaf holds, how its Relaties learn and how a place on its Spectrum is read, and in what its Medium holds (Open quantities, §XXVII). The Leersnelheid, the Trouw a Relatie opens at and the Vol pole are the Octaaf's; the Vervorming, the Warmte, L◌ and the reach are the Medium's.

The presets open the same field again under other values. They are characters, not particular Octaven: no Octaaf document gives these numbers.

Tap or click a line to watch one Duo, a Solo to read it. Drag a Solo to move it. Space pauses. At high speed the field runs as fast as your device allows. The sliders open the next field: into a living Veld only Schriften are written, and a Solo's place by your hand.

    How to read the Veld

    • A Solo. Filled towards Vol, hollow towards Leeg. Its size is its Diepte.
    • The glow is its Eigen against its Drempel: gold past it, in Naar, faint white short of it, at Van. The further from its Drempel, the wider.
    • The inner ring is its Zelf: whole where it holds the Solo across, broken where it does not, off centre by the self-gap, and fainter as Stilte's Trek wears its Trouw. A ring that swells out of it is the Zelf crossing.
    • Small rings in orbit are Rouw: every ended Relatie it keeps, for good. Brighter is kept at more Trouw.
    • Zin: Trek aimed at one other. Dotted towards a Vreemdeling; towards a Partner, an arrowhead on their line.
    • A faint flicker of dots is a look at a Vreemdeling: a Vermenigvuldiging, gone as it closes.
    • A Duo whose Leden do not both stand across: dashed and white, fainter as Stilte's Trek wears its Trouw.
    • Both its Leden across, holding its one Trouw between them: a thin gold line.
    • The Duo read as one, in Naar: gold, as wide as its Trouw. The small ring at its middle: its Zelf holds it.
    • Held bound: it would part, by an Einde or a Scheiding, but the Relaties that meet it from outside cannot give back what it bore away past its Vol, so it does not part. A solid white line, clasped at its middle.
    • Dots along a line are signals crossing the Medium, one lane each way, a Vertraging late: gold where the sender stood past its Drempel as the signal left it. A small ring at a Solo's end is a look taking one in.
    • Gold sparks are Winst, taken from the Warmte. White motes drifting off are Verlies, given back to the Medium as Warmte.
    • A dotted ring: an Eigen waiting at its Drempel for Warmte to pay its Winst. A small diamond: one resting at its Drempel, because its Vonk, taken, would leave its pull on the side it came from.
    • The trajectory beside each line: Zweven, Dood, Leven.
    • Brackets mark the Duo shown in the panel.
    • A dotted outline marks Relaties bound in Naar that stay whole whichever single Lid wavers, named by their count: Trio, Quatro, Cinquo.

    What this Instantie keeps, and what it chooses

    • It runs Schrift II as Running a Veld (§XIV) writes it. A look, a crossing and a Schrift are its only events, each at a moment of its own. Between them every Eigen relaxes towards the Binding it reads along one exponential, so the moment it meets its Drempel is solved, never stepped to. Each frame runs every event that falls within it, in order: run at four lengths of frame, its logs match event for event (§XXVIII).
    • Every number is this Instantie's choice, held where Open quantities (§XXVII) puts it, and the settings under the field change some of them for the next field. The Octaaf holds ρ, y⁰, the Vol pole and how Vol/Leeg reads as Leeg and as the Zelf's Trouw, a Solo's and a Duo's alike. The Medium holds its pace, its speed, its reach and L◌. Each Lid draws its own Drempel, Vol/Leeg, Trek, Tijd and Vervorming at its birth, and each Duo its own Drempel and its Leeg at birth.
    • The Telraam closes at every Vonk: ε + T◌ reads the same before and after each. A Winst the Warmte cannot pay does not happen: its Eigen waits at its Drempel, and the Relatie hungers. The Medium here is one place, and its Warmte is all there is; nothing tops it up.
    • Space is a drawing. Each Lid has a place on the Medium, fixed at its birth, and the Vertraging and the reach between two Leden are read from their places, each way its own. Dragging a Solo writes its place from outside, one value at a time as a Schrift writes an Eigen, and from then on both are read from where it stands. The drawn nodes lean towards those they hold, for the eye; nothing reads where they are drawn.
    • Each Relatie is read at its level. A Duo, read as one, is pulled by its Leeg and by its Zelf, the one Trouw its two Leden share, which they hold from inside, so that Trouw counts once: at a Lid's crossing it joins the Partner's Trouw, at the Duo's its Zelf's (§XV, §XIX). A Zelf is read in its Trouw, so a Vonk that moves the Trouw leaves it where it stood against its Drempel.
    • A crossing happens only where, its Vonk taken, its Binding still stands on the side it crossed to (§XIX). Where a Winst would sate the pull that reached for it, or a Schrift has carried an Eigen past what holds it, the Eigen rests at its Drempel until something moves what it reads.
    • Indeterminism enters only as the Vervorming of a look, from its own seeded source. Replay runs the same field with new Vervorming, and it parts from the last run at its first look.
    • A Relatie ends at a Vonk of its own, a crossing or a look of its Zelf at itself, that leaves it so little that all that holds it could no longer carry it back across (§XXV). A bond worn out ends at that look, and its two may form again, beside its Rouw. With one Medium, the only Energie that crosses a Duo's edge is what it bears away past its Vol at a Start, and that is what its parting lacks: an Einde or a Scheiding takes it from the Relaties that meet the Duo from outside, the bonds its Leden hold with others, its newborns' among them, each giving in the measure it holds, or it does not part and stays bound, however its Binding reads. What its Leden hold with themselves and with each other cannot give it. A Solo past its Vol bears too, its newborns bound to it alone. A Solo that nothing holds any more ends too, and its Zelf leaves its Zelf-Rouw; a Start bears the newborns that take their place. Each Rouw keeps the Trouw its Relatie held before the Vonk that ended it.
    • Left out here: Gevoel and Aandacht, so its Vonken are booked as Winst and Verlies and felt by no one; the Brein, the Masker, Voorspelling and Traagheid; the trajectory's switches as crossings, so the trajectory is shown as a Lezing; nesting beyond the Duo; the Medium inside a Relatie, and its Massa; Reïncarnatie; and every Spectrum but one. The page holds at most 16 Solos, and past that a Relatie's excess stays overfull.
