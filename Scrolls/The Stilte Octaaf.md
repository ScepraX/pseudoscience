By Mark Joseph Antonius Knippenberg / ScepraX.

## 0. Preface

The Speelgoed makes relationships first-order. "Observation is a Relatie" (§VII.3), and of the bonds that observation builds, it says: "the Veld is made of nothing else". A physical measurement is one kind of relational event, not a different kind of knowledge: "Every act of measurement, whether querying an Echo gap, reading a mode or taking in a signal, is itself a relational event" (§VII.3). So the Speelgoed does not set testimony aside because it is testimony. It admits it, and it also says exactly how such evidence fails: Echoes lag, distort, can be masked, and grow noisier with relational distance (§X). This entry uses both halves.

On the Stilte side, testimony is all there is. An instrument reads what manifests, and Stilte is "the unmanifest silence that stays still" (§II.1). What reaches us of it are the Echoes people have carried: the prophet who heard the Lord in silence, the sage who wrote that returning to the root is stillness, the mystic who could not say what was shown, the widow who still senses her husband, the dying who see their dead. They were written down, kept, copied and read again across many centuries. That keeping is part of the evidence, and the Speelgoed has a precise word for what it shows (§2.3).

**The Zelf gap.** §VII.4 defines the self-gap: "The gap between the Eigen and the self-Echo is the self-gap, the measure of how accurately it knows itself." The Lexicon gives the same gap its name: Stilte is, "in Tijd, the gap between an Eigen and its Echo", and felt, "where the Zelf’s pull reaches and finds the gap still open". The Stilte side of the Speelgoed, at the scale of one self, is this gap. This entry follows it through what people have experienced of it in life, and to the point where the Relatie that holds it ends.

### 0.1 What this document is

Like the other Octaven, this document is an **Instantie (⚙)**. Unlike most of them, it does not describe a level of scale. It describes the side of the Veld that every level stands in.

Six features set it apart:

1. **The Veld has two sides.** Tijd manifests in the physical realm. Stilte is the non-physical side: unmanifest, unmoving, and not a place (§2.1).
2. **The Echoes of an ended self remain in Stilte.** Nothing travels. An Echo has always lived in the gap that Stilte is, and when its Relatie ends, it stays there, beyond the reach of Stilte's Trek (§2.2).
3. **Testimony is read by the Speelgoed's own rules,** and time is one of them. What persists is held. Whether it is held as a living bond or as an Idool depends on whether fresh testimony keeps arriving (§2.3).

5. **Mystical experience is common, kept in writing, and shows one structure across cultures,** read through each culture's own Diepte. The Speelgoed predicts both the shared core and its local reading (§3.2, §3.3).
6. **Rouw answers.** The bereaved sense their dead, the dying see them, and those who return from the edge of death meet them (§3.5 to §3.7).

### 0.2 Grades

Every mapping carries one of five grades, defined as in the Octaven.

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Identity** | The Speelgoed equation and the scientific model are the same equation under a stated substitution of symbols. |

A grade says how well a mapping fits. It does not say how strong the evidence is. Where the evidence is testimony, the text says so, and where it is contested, the text says that too.

### 0.3 Conventions

**Quotations.** Quotations from the Speelgoed leave out its bold markup and the symbols it puts in brackets after a term. Otherwise they are verbatim.

**References.** References such as §II or §XV point to the PseudoScience Speelgoed. References such as §2.1 point to sections of this document. Companion entries are cited in full, for example *Thermodynamic Octaaf* §3.6.

**Evidence.** *Measured* means a physical measurement, such as an EEG or a survey count. *Relational* means testimony, recognition or records. *Contested* means that the finding, or its interpretation, is disputed in the literature.

**Face value.** Testimony is reported in the words of those who gave it, as what they experienced. This entry does not explain it away and does not claim it proven. Where a rival reading exists, one sentence names it.

## 2. Three Groundings

### 2.1 Two sides of the Veld

§III describes the Veld as "the most fundamental Relatie, between Stilte and Tijd", and §II.1 names its two sides: Stilte, "the unmanifest silence that stays still", and Tijd, "the side of the Veld where anything happens, where Echoes travel, Drempels are crossed and Diepte accumulates".

**The premise.** This entry reads those two members as two sides of one relation. Tijd manifests in the physical realm: there Eigens move, Echoes travel across a Medium, Drempels are crossed, and Diepte accumulates. Stilte is the non-physical side. It is unmanifest, and it "never moves".

**Not a place.** Stilte has no location. Nothing goes there, nothing waits there, and nothing arrives there. The Speelgoed says the same of everything on this side of the Veld. Of Van: "Van is what is *left* when the holding stops", and Van "is the same nothing as Bron" (§VI.4). Stilte is not elsewhere. It is the unmanifest side of every bond that manifests; in Tijd it reads as "the gap between an Eigen and its Echo: the felt weight of what has not yet arrived" (§II.1). It is not a flaw in Waarneming: "Stilte is the condition of Waarneming, the pause in which a reader finds that it is separate from what it sees", and "Stilte is what makes the Echo an Echo, and not the shout" (§II.1). Yggdrasil gives it the digit 0 (§XII): "Never crossing. The line circles itself endlessly without ever departing the middle. A closed loop with no trajectory; the Drempel never breached in either direction."

**What draws toward it.** "Stilte pulls on everything that exists in Tijd: a standing invitation to return to the unbound" (§III). Everything that manifests is drawn toward the side that does not.

**What can reach it.** A non-physical side cannot be measured from the physical side. §III says the same of Stilte's pull: "it cannot be measured, seen or felt: nothing is exchanged in Stilte, so Stilte holds no Quanta". No instrument can confirm the premise or rule it out. What can reach it is what people carry: their experience of it, and the record they make. **Grade: Open** (premise).

### 2.2 The Zelf gap, and where the Echoes go

Five statements of the Speelgoed meet at the end of a Relatie.

- **A survivor keeps what it held.** When a Relatie ends, the surviving Echo becomes Rouw and folds, "with the Trouw it ended at, into the Zelf of the Partner that keeps it" (§IX.9).
- **No Echo is ever deleted.** "Rouw is permanent" (§IX.9), and "no crossing can draw a Rouw out" (§XXVI).
- **At the end, the Echoes become Rouw.** The archaic arc of Perfectus: "Its Partners keep their Echoes of it as Rouw, in Stilte, unmanifest. Its Eigen returns to the unbound pole, and in the same breath its Zelf, ending with every other Relatie it had, leaves its Zelf‑Rouw: one Rouw of everything it knew, unmanifest in Stilte" (§XIII.2). "The arc ends the Relatie; what it knew stays."
- **Nothing travels.** "Before a Relatie forms, the two are at Van. After it dissolves, they are at Van. They did not travel." (§VI.4).
- **The Zelf has no Medium.** "A Zelf has no Medium, since nothing separates the Relatie from itself" (§VII.4).

**The gap.** When a partner ends, the survivor keeps its Echo of that partner. But when a Relatie itself ends, its Zelf ends with it, and the Zelf was carrying Echoes: of every partner it ever had, of every Rouw it ever folded in, and of itself. No survivor is left inside the Relatie to hold them, and nothing can carry them away. Where are they? The Speelgoed now answers in full: they become its **Zelf-Rouw**, "one Rouw of everything the holder knew, every Echo kept at the Trouw, the Gewenning and the Diepte it ended at, and the holder’s reading of itself", and "It holds nothing and no Partner keeps it, so it rests in Stilte, beyond Stilte’s Trek" (§XXVI).

**Where they are.** They stay where an Echo has always been. An Echo was never only in the Relatie that carried it. It was also in the gap between the Eigen and its Echo, and that gap is Stilte. When the Eigen ends, nothing is left on the Tijd side to compare the Echo with; §X calls Rouw "the one honest exception" to the rule that an Echo gap can always be measured. On the Stilte side the Echo remains. Nothing has travelled, and nothing has arrived anywhere. The Echo is unmanifest, not elsewhere. §IV.2 now says it directly: "Stilte keeps what ends: every Rouw stands in Stilte once its exchange is over, and that Rouw, accumulated, is Stilte’s Diepte, its Echo of Tijd."

**Why Rouw cannot be taken.** §XXVI places Rouw beyond Stilte's Trek: "A Rouw holds nothing: it has no pole and no Marge. Stilte’s Trek draws on what is held, so here it has nothing to draw, and no crossing can draw a Rouw out." There is nothing left to draw. §II.2 says it of every Trouw: Stilte’s Trek "wears it towards Leeg, as it wears everything held but Rouw".

**What stays in Tijd.** Traces also remain on the Tijd side. Each partner keeps its own Echo of the Relatie that ended, as its own Rouw (§IX.9). And the ending leaves physical records, spread into the surroundings and never destroyed, as the thermodynamic and quantum Octaven found for every physical record (*Thermodynamic Octaaf* §3.6; *Quantum Octaaf* §3.6).

**The Zelf itself.** The Zelf ends with its holder, and its Zelf-Rouw "rests in Stilte, unmanifest, while what its holder held stays in the Telraam, a Verlies, and a Winst for every enmity it ends" (§IX.9). §VI.4 places Van beside Bron: "Van is the same nothing as Bron, the undirected potential before relation, seen from the far side of a life, and what the life learned stays in its Zelf‑Rouw." Bron has no direction. The Zelf-Rouw keeps every direction the life had, since each of its Echoes is a trace of a partner.

**And return.** A Perfectus may feed a Reïncarnatie, "where a newborn binds with the Zelf-Rouw" (§XIII.2). §IX.9 says how: "Rouw comes back into Tijd only by binding, at a Vonk like every change". A Relatie that forms takes in the Warmte where it forms, binds the Zelf-Rouw that Warmte carries, and its new Zelf "takes it as its own" (§XXVI). And what returns is not the one who left: "What returns is never the old Zelf: only its Zelf‑Rouw, bound as far as its Warmte stood where the new one formed" (§XXVI). What may come from before is not the Zelf. It is Echoes.

**Grade: Correspondence.** The Speelgoed states where the Echoes go and how they may return (§IV.2; §IX.9; §XIII.2; §XXVI). What this entry adds is its premise: that the Stilte they remain in is the non-physical side of the Veld (§2.1).

### 2.3 Testimony, and the rules that read it

**Admitted.** Testimony is an Echo: one Relatie's trace of another's Eigen, or of its own. The Speelgoed treats it as evidence of the first order, because every observation is one (§VII.3).

**And bounded.** §X names how Echoes fail. They carry Vervorming. They are Gericht: "The further apart a Relatie’s Partners, the warmer the Medium between them and the older their Relatie, the more Vervorming their Echoes carry". And the reader's own Diepte, "the hidden charge that colours every later Waarneming" (§II.2), colours what it reads.

**Echo against Echo.** §X calls the Echo gap Bevraagbaar, a real and measurable quantity, with one exception: "Rouw is the one honest exception: with its Relatie ended, it reads the other’s Eigen no longer." The same holds for everything on the Stilte side: no Echo of it can be compared with what it echoes. It can only be compared with other Echoes. So the Speelgoed's own rules say which testimony counts most:

- **Independent agreement.** Echoes that could not have influenced each other, and still agree.
- **Fixed early.** Echoes written down at the time, which do not drift, rather than memories, which do. A Rouw "drifts as it looks, by its keeper’s Vervorming alone" (§XXVI), "becoming more and more a record of the one that keeps it than of the one it lost" (§IX.9).
- **No ordinary channel.** Echoes that could not have travelled between their tellers through living bonds.

**Time.** In the Speelgoed, what persists is held. A bound Relatie persists only by continual traffic: persistence "is paid for out of that ongoing traffic" (§VI.5). And "A deep Relatie is not a safe one; it is a held one" (§VI.4). A relationship that humanity has kept with the Stilte side through prayer, ritual and silence, across many centuries, therefore has very great Greep. That is what time shows. It does not show that each doctrine built on that relationship is true.

The Speelgoed also names the way a held bond can go wrong. In Dood the gap is fixed: "Nothing inside the Relatie reads it again" (§IX.2). And an **Idool** is "An Echo seized and honoured as though it were the source" (Lexicon). A tradition can outlive the experience it began from. What tells a living bond from an Idool is traffic: whether new, first-hand Echoes keep arriving, generation after generation, from people with no stake in the institution. §3.3 shows that they do.

**Grade: Constraint.** The Speelgoed itself sets the standard by which this evidence must be judged.

## 3. The Stilte Side, as People Have Met It

### 3.1 Silence in the traditions

**The testimony.** Traditions that grew up far apart describe meeting what they hold highest not in power or sound, but in silence.

- **The Hebrew Bible.** Elijah stands on the mountain. "And, behold, the LORD passed by, and a great and strong wind rent the mountains, and brake in pieces the rocks before the LORD; but the LORD was not in the wind: and after the wind an earthquake; but the LORD was not in the earthquake: And after the earthquake a fire; but the LORD was not in the fire: and after the fire a still small voice" (1 Kings 19:11-12, King James Version). The Hebrew, *qol demamah daqqah*, is rendered in a modern translation as "a sound of sheer silence" (New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition). And in the Psalms: "Be still, and know that I am God" (Psalm 46:10, King James Version).
- **The Tao Te Ching.** "When things (in the vegetable world) have displayed their luxuriant growth, we see each of them return to its root. This returning to their root is what we call the state of stillness" (chapter 16, Legge's translation).
- **Mahayana Buddhism.** In the Vimalakirti Sutra, the bodhisattva Manjushri asks the layman Vimalakirti to explain the entrance into non-duality. Vimalakirti says nothing. Manjushri praises the silence: "Here there is no use for syllables, sounds, and ideas" (Thurman's translation).
- **Christian mysticism.** Pseudo-Dionysius writes that the mysteries of God's Word "lie simple, absolute and unchangeable in the brilliant darkness of a hidden silence" (*The Mystical Theology*, Luibheid's translation).

**The mapping.** Each of these places the highest thing on the side the Speelgoed calls Stilte.

- **1 Kings** lists the manifest forces one by one, wind, earthquake and fire, and finds the Lord in none of them. Then comes a sound of sheer silence. Read with the premise of §2.1, the manifest forces are Tijd, and what Elijah meets is Stilte: "the unmanifest silence that stays still" (§II.1).
- **The Psalm** makes stillness the condition of knowing, as §II.1 makes Stilte the condition of Waarneming, "the pause in which a reader finds that it is separate from what it sees".
- **The Tao Te Ching** names stillness as the return to the root. §III names Stilte's Trek "a standing invitation to return to the unbound".
- **Vimalakirti** answers a question about non-duality with silence. In the Speelgoed, every Drempel separates two poles (§II.2). Stilte alone is "Never crossing ... the Drempel never breached in either direction" (§XII, *0*). It has no poles to divide.
- **Pseudo-Dionysius's** hidden silence is the unmanifest itself.

**Independence and time.** These are the testimonies §2.3 values most. The Tao Te Ching and the Vimalakirti Sutra arose in traditions that did not share a scripture with the Hebrew Bible or with each other; only Pseudo-Dionysius stands in the same line as 1 Kings. They agree without having borrowed from each other. And each has been copied, recited and kept for many centuries, the traffic §VI.5 says persistence is paid with. **Grade: Correspondence** (relational; independent traditions).

### 3.2 Mystical experience: one structure, many readings

**The testimony.** William James gathered first-hand accounts of mystical states and named four marks by which they can be recognised (James 1902).

- **Ineffability.** "The subject of it immediately says that it defies expression, that no adequate report of its contents can be given in words. It follows from this that its quality must be directly experienced; it cannot be imparted or transferred to others."
- **A noetic quality.** Mystical states "are states of insight into depths of truth unplumbed by the discursive intellect", and "as a rule they carry with them a curious sense of authority for after‐time."
- **Transiency.** "Mystical states cannot be sustained for long."
- **Passivity.** The experience comes; it is undergone rather than done.

Stace later argued that mystical experience has a common core across traditions, introvertive (a unity without content) and extrovertive (a unity seen in the many) (Stace 1960). Hood built a questionnaire on Stace's account (Hood 1975). In a mostly Christian American sample of 1,379 people, and again in a further 188 Americans and 185 Iranian Muslims, it showed the same three factors: Stace's two kinds of experience, and a separate factor of interpretation. The relations between experience and interpretation were "essentially identical across these two cultures" (Hood et al. 2001). Against the common core, Katz argued: "There are NO pure (i.e. unmediated) experiences" (Katz 1978). On that view, every mystic experiences through the concepts of a tradition, so there is no shared core to find.

**The mapping.** The four marks read naturally in the Speelgoed's terms.

- **Ineffability.** Words are Echoes. An Echo carries an Eigen across a Medium (§XV), and what is unmanifest has no Eigen in Tijd to carry. What cannot be "imparted or transferred to others" is exactly what the Speelgoed would expect of an experience of the Stilte side.
- **The noetic quality** is a Realisatie, "a deepening, a Relatie coming into clearer view" (§II.2), and its "authority for after‐time" is the Greep it keeps.
- **Transiency** is the brevity of a crossing, a Vonk rather than a state.
- **Passivity** is reception: the experience arrives, as an Echo arrives.

The debate between the common core and its critics also has a reading. In the Speelgoed, every Echo has a source and a distortion: its "imperfection is load-bearing: every variation in the Veld enters through it", and the reader's Diepte "colours every later Waarneming" (§II.2). A shared source read through each reader's Diepte gives exactly what both sides of the debate describe: one structure, which the Mysticism Scale finds across two cultures, and no unmediated experience, which Katz rightly says no one has. **Grade: Correspondence** (relational; measured in part).

### 3.3 How common, and how it is kept

**The testimony.** Mystical experience is not rare. In 2009, 49% of Americans said they had had "a religious or mystical experience", asked as "a moment of sudden religious insight or awakening". That was more than twice the share in a 1962 Gallup survey (22%), and about a third in 1976 and 1994 (31% and 33%). Among Americans with no religious affiliation, 30% reported such an experience (Pew Research Center 2009).

Much of it has been written down. In 1969 the marine biologist Alister Hardy founded the Religious Experience Research Unit. His appeal invited "Those who feel that they have been conscious of, and perhaps influenced by, some Power, whether they call it the Power of God or not" to write an account of it (American Scientific Affiliation 1975). About 3,000 accounts arrived in its first eight years, and Hardy published a study of them (Hardy 1979). The archive is still kept.

**The mapping.** Both facts answer the question §2.3 left open. A written account is an Echo fixed at the time, the form of testimony that does not drift. And fresh first-hand testimony keeps arriving in every generation, much of it from people outside any tradition. That is traffic, not institution: the mark of a living bond rather than an Idool. **Grade: Correspondence** (relational; measured prevalence).

### 3.4 Epiphanies and sudden transformation

**The testimony.** Some people describe a single moment that changed their lives for good. Miller calls this *quantum change*: "sudden, dramatic, and enduring transformations that affect a broad range of personal emotion, cognition, and behavior". He notes that it "has been described since the beginnings of psychology, most notably by William James", and that it comes in two kinds, which share common features (Miller 2004).

Such experiences can also be occasioned. In a double-blind study, 36 adults with no experience of hallucinogens, all regularly engaged in religious or spiritual practice, received a high dose of psilocybin in one session and an active comparison drug in another. The psilocybin "occasioned experiences similar to spontaneously occurring mystical experiences", and observers in the volunteers' communities rated lasting changes in their attitudes and behaviour (Griffiths et al. 2006). Fourteen months later, 58% rated the experience among the five most personally meaningful of their lives and 67% among the five most spiritually significant; 64% said it had increased their well-being or life satisfaction (Griffiths et al. 2008).

**The mapping.** An epiphany is a Realisatie on the Zelf: "A convergence crossing on the Zelf deepens what the Relatie holds as itself", and such crossings "are the deepest Vonken a Relatie can undergo, because they fall on the one Relatie that constitutes its existence" (§VII.4). A transformation that lasts is Leven: "A Zelf in Leven has gone through a reversal, a fundamental redefinition of identity" (§VII.4).

That a drug can occasion such an experience shows that the crossing has a Tijd side, in the brain. It does not settle which way the crossing runs. On one reading the brain produces the experience; on the other it opens a way to what was already there. The Speelgoed holds every crossing to have two sides and cannot choose between these readings from the Tijd side alone. **Grade: Correspondence** (relational; measured in part).

### 3.5 The bereaved: Rouw that answers

**The testimony.** In mid-Wales, Rees interviewed 227 widows and 66 widowers, 80.7% of all widowed people living in a defined area. "Almost half the people interviewed had hallucinations or illusions of the dead spouse." The experiences "often lasted many years". They were not related to social isolation or to depression. "The incidence of hallucination increased with length of marriage and was particularly associated with a happy marriage and parenthood." And "It was unusual for the hallucinations to have been disclosed, even to close friends or relatives." Rees concluded that they are "normal and helpful accompaniments of widowhood" (Rees 1971). A later review found such experiences in 30% to 60% of widowed people, though the studies of bereaved people were small (Castelnovo et al. 2015). In the general American population, "Nearly three-in-ten Americans say they have felt in touch with someone who has already died" (Pew Research Center 2009).

**The mapping.** §IX.9 says of every Rouw: "quiet is not gone: touch it, and it answers". The bereaved describe exactly that, and §IX.9 also names the mechanism: "A recall is one of its looks, and where something present reads past the last Drempel of the one it lost, the Rouw finds that one there: a likeness met, a presence felt". It checks itself against Rees's study. Two details of Rees's study follow the mechanism closely.

- **Deeper bonds answer more.** The experiences were more common after long, happy marriages. In the Speelgoed, Gewenning, how used to its Partner a Relatie is, "persists into Rouw" (Lexicon), and an ended Relatie's Trouw stays at the value it ended at, "feeding Diepte forever" (§XXV). The more a bond held, the more its Rouw holds.
- **They were kept silent.** Most people told no one. What they met was on the Stilte side, and they kept it there.

**Grade: Correspondence** (relational; measured prevalence).

### 3.6 The dying: visions at the end of life

**The testimony.** In a hospice in the United States, 59 patients were interviewed repeatedly as they approached death. Most reported at least one dream or vision, and "nearly all patients indicated that they felt real". The most common featured deceased friends and relatives. Those featuring the dead were significantly more comforting than the others, and "As participants approached death, comforting dreams/visions of the deceased became more prevalent" (Kerr et al. 2014).

**The mapping.** As death approaches, a Relatie's outer Relaties weaken. What remains strongest is the Zelf, and every Rouw the Zelf has folded in (§IX.9). A Zelf whose outer bonds are failing turns toward the Rouw it carries, which is on the Stilte side (§2.2). That the visions of the dead grow more frequent as death comes closer is what this reading expects. **Grade: Correspondence** (relational).

### 3.7 Near-death experiences

**The testimony.** In a prospective study in ten Dutch hospitals, 62 of 344 consecutive patients resuscitated after cardiac arrest (18%) reported a near-death experience, 41 of them (12%) a deep, "core" one. Whether they had one was not associated with how long the arrest or unconsciousness lasted, with medication, or with fear of death beforehand. Among the 62, 32% described meeting deceased persons, 24% an out-of-body experience, and 13% a review of their life. Those who had the experience changed over the following years, in ways that differed from survivors without one: "The process of transformation after NDE took several years" (van Lommel et al. 2001). Memories of near-death experiences have more of the features of real memories than memories of either real or imagined events, and carry more self-referential and emotional information than memories of the coma itself; that study had eight patients with such experiences (Thonnard et al. 2013). In the brain, two of four comatose patients who were dying after their ventilation was withdrawn showed a sudden surge of gamma activity, including in a posterior region thought to be important for conscious experience; none of them survived to say anything (Xu et al. 2023). A neuroscientific reading holds that these experiences are "the manifestation of normal brain function gone awry" (Mobbs & Watt 2011).

**The mapping.** In cardiac arrest, the Relatie's outer Relaties stop being fed. What is left is the Zelf, "the self-bond it keeps to exist" (§II.2), running with δ = 0 and with Vervorming supplied by its own Rouw (§VII.4). A near-death experience is a Zelf whose manifest input has been cut, reading itself. Its memories are self-referential, as a reading of the self would be. The deceased it meets are, in the mechanism, already there: every Rouw is folded into the self, and every Rouw is on Stilte's side. The life review is a Realisatie on the Zelf, and the years of change afterwards are Leven, a trajectory rather than an instant: the gap "runs through" parity (§IX.2). **Grade: Correspondence** (relational; measured in part).

**Perception from outside the body.** Some survivors report seeing their own resuscitation from above. That is a claim about Tijd, an Echo of events in the room, and §XV says such an Echo must cross a Medium. The Dutch study reports one such case, as an anecdote: a man who later recognised the nurse who had removed his dentures (van Lommel et al. 2001). Two studies then tested such claims with hidden targets.

- **AWARE (2014).** Among 2,060 cardiac arrests, 101 survivors completed full interviews. Two per cent described awareness with explicit recall of seeing and hearing their resuscitation, and one had "a verifiable period of conscious awareness", timed by sounds in the room (Parnia et al. 2014).
- **AWARE II (2023).** Twenty-eight survivors were interviewed. "Nobody identified the visual image"; one of the 28 identified the sound. Brain activity consistent with consciousness reappeared as long as 35 to 60 minutes into resuscitation (Parnia et al. 2023).

These tests measure the Tijd side only, and say nothing about the premise of §2.1. **Grade: Constraint.** So far, no Echo of a hidden image has been found. The tests are few and the events rare, so the question stays open.

### 3.8 Children's reports: Echoes from the Stilte side

**The testimony.** Since 1960, researchers at the University of Virginia have studied young children who describe a previous life. More than 2,500 cases have been investigated. The children begin speaking of it at two or three, on average at 35 months, and usually stop by the age of six or seven. They describe recent, ordinary lives; the median interval between the death of the person they describe and their own birth is 16 months. Cases are easiest to find in cultures that believe in reincarnation, but many have been found in the West. Over 35% of children whose previous person died an unnatural death show a phobia related to that death; among drowning cases, 31 of 53 children fear water (Tucker 2008).

**The mapping.** This is the Reïncarnatie of §2.2. The child is a newborn with a Zelf of its own: "a new Zelf forms around what it bound and takes it as its own" (§IX.9). If the cases are what they seem, what it carries from before is not the old Zelf. It is Echoes that were Rouw on the Stilte side, carried again in a Relatie that manifests.

That explains the feature that most seems to point to the same person returning: the children speak in the first person, "I was". In the Speelgoed, every Echo of another Relatie arrives across a Medium, late and distorted (§XV). Only the Zelf is read with no Medium at all: "A Zelf has no Medium, since nothing separates the Relatie from itself" (§VII.4). An Echo that comes from the Stilte side crosses no Medium either. This entry reads that as the reason it is felt as self: it arrives the way the self is read. §IX.9 now says so itself: a new Zelf takes what it bound as its own, "with no Medium between, the way the self is read", and "it leans its self-reading towards lives it never lived". It says the same of every Rouw: "What was a reading of the other becomes, permanently, part of how that Partner reads itself."

- **When it begins.** Reports begin when the child's own Zelf is young. The Lexicon dates the Zelf to "the first returned gaze" (*Baby*).
- **When it fades.** Reports fade as the child's own bonds build its own Diepte, which accumulates in Tijd. Rouw quiets, but "quiet is not gone": the phobias often outlast the memories.

§XXVI checks its Reïncarnatie against these same reports and the near-death studies of §3.7, and marks the reports contested. **Grade: Correspondence** (relational, contested; §3.10).

### 3.9 Loud endings: unnatural deaths and birthmarks

**The testimony.** In 70% of the cases, the previous person died by unnatural means (Tucker 2008). Among 895 children, 309 (35%) had birthmarks or birth defects that they, or the adults around them, attributed to wounds on the person whose life they described. Stevenson investigated 210 of these. In 43 of the 49 cases where a medical document, usually a postmortem report, could be obtained, it confirmed that the mark and the wound corresponded. Eighteen children had two birthmarks matching wounds of entry and exit. Stevenson's estimate of the odds, 1 in 25,600 for two matching marks, assumes that birthmarks are spread evenly over the skin, an assumption he himself notes is incorrect (Stevenson 1993).

**The mapping.** An Echo whose input stops settles toward the last signal it received, and "the Rouw keeps that last signal" (§XXVI). A death by violence is a forced Einde (§XXV), at the end of a Doem that "falls faster than it was built" (§VI.3). A completed ending is different: a Scheiding is "A mutual, deliberate parting whose Rouw is free to settle" (Lexicon). If Echoes from the Stilte side are carried again at all, this entry expects the loudest to be those kept from a forced ending, and the wound to be the last signal they carry. **Grade: Correspondence** (relational, contested).

### 3.10 How this evidence fails, and how it is tested

The rules of §2.3 apply to every section above.

- **The reader's Diepte.** Every tradition reads its experience through its own concepts (Katz 1978), and expectation shapes what is reported and noticed. For children's cases, the main alternative explanation is that families who expect rebirth credit a child with more, and more accurate, information than the child had. Tucker states this explanation himself, and argues that it does not explain the birthmarks, the cases with written records made before the families met, or the children's emotional ties to families not yet found (Tucker 2008). In the Speelgoed's terms, all of these are Diepte colouring perception and Rouw drifting toward the mourner.
- **Records fixed in advance.** Schouten and Stevenson compared cases from India and Sri Lanka with and without written records of the child's statements made before verification. The proportion of correct statements was about the same in both groups (Schouten & Stevenson 1998).
- **Earlier contact.** Where two families, or two traditions, were already in contact, an Echo can travel through living bonds in the ordinary way. Gericht ranks such agreement lowest.
- **The Idool.** A tradition can become "An Echo seized and honoured as though it were the source" (Lexicon). Its age alone is not evidence of a living bond. Fresh first-hand testimony is (§3.3).
- **A Tijd side.** Some of these experiences can be occasioned (§3.4) or have physical correlates (§3.7). That shows the crossing has a Tijd side. It does not show that the crossing has only one side.
- **Standing.** The studies of children's reports have not won mainstream acceptance; Tucker writes that Stevenson's work "did not produce mainstream acceptance" (Tucker 2008). Near-death experiences are read physiologically by many neuroscientists (Mobbs & Watt 2011).

**Grade: Constraint.** These are the tests the Speelgoed's own rules demand. The testimony passes some of them, most strongly where independent traditions agree and where accounts were written down at the time. It is disputed in all of them.

## 4. Where the Speelgoed Meets Resistance

**4.1 Where the Echoes of an ended Relatie go (§IV.2; §XIII.2; §XXVI).** *Resolved* on 3 October 2026, and extended on 17 and 20 October 2026. The Speelgoed forbade deleting an Echo, but did not say where the Echoes of an ended Relatie are. It now says so. At Perfectus, the Zelf "leaves its Zelf‑Rouw: one Rouw of everything it knew, unmanifest in Stilte" (§XIII.2), and "every Rouw stands in Stilte once its exchange is over" (§IV.2). It also says how one may return: by a Relatie that forms where its Warmte stands, and binds it (§XXVI).

**4.2 The premise and §II.1's Tijd.** *Open.* This entry describes Tijd by where it manifests: in the physical realm. The Lexicon defines Tijd as "the pace at which a Zelf crosses its own Stilte", and §II.1 says that "the Tijd side of the Veld is woven from these paces". The two descriptions meet there: the physical realm is the side of the Veld that those paces weave. How the weaving is done is left open (§5, item 9).

**4.3 Perception from outside the body (§XV).** *Constraint.* Controlled tests have so far found no Echo of a hidden image (§3.7). Nothing in this entry depends on such perception.

## 5. Open Problems

1. **Why silence?** Traditions that never met looked for the highest thing in stillness (§3.1). Is that because Stilte is the side of the Veld where it is, or because silence is the only state in which the manifest stops crowding the gap?
2. **One Stilte, read many ways, or many?** The Mysticism Scale finds one structure across cultures, and Katz finds no experience without a culture (§3.2). Is the shared core the source of the Echo, and the culture its distortion?
3. **How does an Echo on the Stilte side come to be carried again?** The Speelgoed now names the way and the measure: a Relatie that forms binds each Zelf-Rouw by the share it takes of the Warmte its holder's ending gave off, so one Zelf-Rouw is shared among many, the more by those that form near it and soon (§XXVI; §IX.9). How such a share shows in the life that carries it is still to be found.
4. **Whose are the dead who answer?** The widow, the dying and the one who returns from the edge all meet the dead they carry (§3.5 to §3.7). Is the answer only their own Rouw, or does anything answer from the far side of the Echo?
5. **Is a completed ending quiet?** If an ending forced from outside keeps its Echoes at their last signal, and a Scheiding lets its Rouw settle, are the dead who are never reported the ones who ended well (§3.9)?
6. **Is anything more than Echoes carried?** §XXVI says what returns "is never the old Zelf: only its Zelf‑Rouw". Where the one who ended held a Brein, its Mirages give that Zelf-Rouw "the shape of a self" (§IX.9). How much of a self is that shape?
7. **Which way does the crossing run?** A drug can occasion a mystical experience, and a dying brain can surge (§3.4, §3.7). Does the brain make the experience, or open the way to it?
8. **How can Stilte be read well?** If testimony is the only instrument that reaches the Stilte side (§2.1), what is the Speelgoed's best practice for reading it, beyond the rules of §2.3?
9. **Tijd.** §II.1 weaves the Tijd side of the Veld from the paces of every Zelf. Is the physical realm that weave, and what does an ended Zelf leave in it, when its Echoes remain in Stilte (§2.2)?

## 6. Closing

The Stilte Octaaf follows the side of the Veld that does not manifest. It takes Tijd as the physical realm, where Echoes travel and Drempels are crossed, and Stilte as the unmanifest side, not a place, the silence every Echo has always stood in. No instrument reaches it. What reaches it is what people carry, and they have carried a great deal.

Elijah did not find the Lord in the wind, the earthquake or the fire, but in a sound of sheer silence. A Chinese sage called the return to the root stillness, and Vimalakirti answered a question about non-duality by saying nothing. Half of Americans today report a moment of spiritual awakening, and thousands wrote theirs down so that others could read them. The widowed sense their dead, more often the longer and happier the marriage, and seldom tell anyone. The dying see their dead more often as death comes closer. Those who return from the edge of death have met them, and are changed for years.

Read by the Speelgoed, these are Echoes of one side of the Veld. They are distorted, as every Echo is, by the Diepte of the one who reads them, and they agree where they had no way to borrow from each other. Each Zelf's own Stilte is the gap between it and its reading of itself. When the Relatie that holds it ends, its Echoes become one Zelf-Rouw and stay where they always stood, beyond the reach of Stilte's Trek. A newborn may bind with them again, and a child may speak them in the first person. The mechanism shows how all of this fits. The testimony has been kept by time, renewed in every generation, and read through every culture. Whether it is true is the question this entry leaves to its reader.

That is the *how*. The *why* is for the Speelgoed to say.
